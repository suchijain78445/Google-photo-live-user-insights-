▸ CORE PROBLEM STATEMENT

Users remember a photo through its context, purpose, or emotional memory — but Google Photos requires a date, location, person, or object keyword.

When users can't provide those structured metadata anchors, they fall back to manual scrolling through years of photos — a process that is time-consuming, unreliable, and increasingly frustrating as libraries grow.
